How to read this page
Bottom line
How many picks clear the discipline bar (edge at least 3%, EV above 0, confidence at least "low"). PASS means nothing does; skipping is a valid decision.
BET / watchlist
BET rows have a suggested stake. Watchlist rows are shown for context with no stake.
Est. win %
Our estimate of the chance the bet wins: the market's no-vig probability (vig removed from the book's prices, averaged over books when several exist). It is an estimate, not a guarantee.
Implied %
The win chance the price itself implies, including the book's margin.
Edge
Est. win % minus implied %. Positive means the price is better than the estimate says it should be.
EV / $1
Expected profit per dollar staked if the estimate is right. +0.05 means 5 cents per dollar on average, with a lot of variance.
Stake
Quarter-Kelly of your bankroll, capped at 2% per bet, 2% per game, and 10% per week across everything.
Confidence
none = one source only; low = few books; medium = 4+ books; high = a sharp book plus 3+ books. Keyless live data rarely goes above "none"/"low".
Ratings % and FPI %
Reference numbers from a simple team-ratings model and ESPN's matchup predictor. They are not used in EV. When they disagree strongly with the market, they are usually the ones that are wrong.
Parlays
Several bets that must all win. The book's margin compounds, legs are assumed independent, and a real parlay must be placed at one book, so treat them as entertainment unless EV is clearly positive.
Track record
Paper trading only: picks logged each week and graded from final scores. No real money.
