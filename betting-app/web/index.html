5 parlays (3-5 legs)

Parlays multiply each leg's sportsbook margin, so they usually carry more vig than the same bets placed singly. Legs are from different games and assumed independent (no same-game legs). Best prices may be at different books; a real parlay is placed at one book and pays less than shown. Stake cap 0.5% of bankroll.
